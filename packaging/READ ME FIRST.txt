Wallpaper Manager 3
===================

A Windows 10/11 app for your desktop and lock screen: a library of pictures and
videos, live wallpapers behind the desktop icons (videos, videos from YouTube
and other sites, interactive web pages and shaders, also reacting to music),
the real night sky and the real Earth as live wallpapers, widgets, ambient
sound, lock screen pictures, playlists and a daily schedule.


Starting it
-----------
Installer: run WallpaperManager-<version>-setup.exe. No administrator rights
are needed.

Zip: unzip the whole WallpaperManager folder somewhere permanent, e.g.
Documents\WallpaperManager (don't run it from inside the zip file), then
double-click WallpaperManager.exe.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen), because the app
isn't signed by a publisher. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

Coming from an older version? Your library, playlists and schedule are kept.
The library is upgraded the first time the new version starts; a copy of the
old one is saved next to it.


Getting started
---------------
- Library -> "Import files" / "Import folder" adds JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP,
  MP4, WEBM, MKV and MOV files (they are not copied or moved). You can also
  drag files, folders or links onto the window.
- Library -> "Add from..." -> "A video link": paste a YouTube (or Vimeo, ...)
  link or a plain .mp4/.webm/.m3u8 link. Stream it, or save it for offline use.
- Library -> "Add from..." -> "A web page or web wallpaper": a folder with an
  index.html, an .html file, a .zip (wallpapers made for Lively work too) or a
  website. Wallpapers with settings show them in the Library details.
- Library -> "Add from..." -> "Wallpaper Engine": video and web wallpapers from
  your Steam workshop subscriptions.
- Discover: search wallhaven.cc and add pictures with one click.
- Library: pick a picture -> "Make it alive..." for a 3D photo, day and night
  with the real sun, the real weather (rain, snow, fog), layers of effects,
  colors that follow the music, a zoom, parallax, rain or snow.
- Library: "Night sky" is the sky over your city right now (stars, the moon,
  the planets, the Milky Way); "Live Earth" is the Earth with yesterday's
  satellite picture, day and night as they are now, and the space station.
  Both use the city picked in Schedule (or a guess from your time zone).
- Desktop -> Widgets: a clock, the weather, the song that plays, system use and
  a calendar behind your icons.
- Customize: make animated shader wallpapers (Shadertoy-style GLSL) with a live
  preview, or start from one of the samples.
- Desktop: click a picture or a live wallpaper to use it. With more than one
  display you can give each display its own wallpaper.
- Lock Screen: click a picture, or a video to choose one of its frames.
- Settings -> Appearance: light or dark mode and the app's color (nine colors
  or any color you like); optionally the taskbar in the wallpaper's color.
- Settings -> Ambient sound: rain when it rains outside, crickets on warm
  nights, or a sound you choose. Also in the tray menu.
- Settings -> Screensaver: your wallpaper or a playlist as the screensaver.
- Closing the window keeps the app running in the system tray (near the clock).
  Right-click the tray icon for Next / Random wallpaper, Pause / Stop and Exit.


Good to know
------------
- Before changing anything, the app saves your current wallpaper and lock
  screen. "Restore original" on the Desktop and Lock Screen pages puts them back.
- The first web, shader, Night sky or Live Earth wallpaper downloads the web
  engine (Chromium, about 150 MB) into the app's data folder. 3D photos
  download a small AI model (27 MB) the first time.
- Interactive wallpapers react to the mouse on the empty desktop; your icons,
  menus and selections keep working as usual.
- Audio-reactive wallpapers listen to what your PC plays. The sound is only
  analysed while such a wallpaper is visible; nothing is recorded or sent.
- Live wallpapers pause automatically while a game or other fullscreen app
  runs, while windows cover the desktop, or while the PC is locked.
- If another live wallpaper app (Wallpaper Engine, Lively, ...) is running, it
  may cover this app's wallpapers - pause or close it.
- New versions: Settings tells you when one is out; "Install now" updates
  the app and keeps your library and settings.
- If YouTube links stop working, use Settings -> "Update yt-dlp".
- Something wrong? Settings -> "Save diagnostics..." makes a zip with the logs
  to attach to a bug report.


Removing it
-----------
1. If you turned on "Start with Windows", the screensaver or the taskbar
   color in Settings, turn them off first (this puts your own back).
2. Use "Restore original" on the Desktop and Lock Screen pages if you want
   your old wallpaper and lock screen back.
3. Right-click the tray icon -> Exit.
4. Uninstall it from Windows Settings -> Apps (installer), or delete the
   WallpaperManager folder (zip).
5. To remove your library and settings too, delete the data folder:
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\WallpaperManager   (paste this into the File Explorer address bar)
