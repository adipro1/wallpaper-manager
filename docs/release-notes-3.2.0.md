Wallpaper Manager 3.2 "Desktop": widgets behind your icons, a taskbar in the wallpaper's color, and your wallpaper as the screensaver. Your library, playlists, schedule and settings carry over.

## New
- **Desktop widgets** (Desktop > Widgets): a clock, the weather, the song that plays, CPU / memory / GPU use and a calendar, drawn behind the desktop icons over any wallpaper: a picture, a video or a web page. Drag them into place on a picture of your screen, make them bigger or smaller, with or without a dark card. They take little: each one is redrawn only when what it shows changes.
- **Taskbar in the wallpaper's color** (Settings > Appearance): switches on Windows' own "accent color on Start and taskbar" and feeds it the wallpaper's color. Switching it off puts your setting back. (Windows shows it when its mode is dark.)
- **Screensaver** (Settings > Screensaver): Wallpaper Manager as the Windows screensaver. It shows your wallpaper — a live one keeps playing, a picture slowly zooms — or a playlist's pictures as a slideshow, with the time. Moving the mouse or pressing a key ends it; "lock on resume" works as with any screensaver. Your previous screensaver comes back when you switch it off.
- The weather now includes the temperature (for the weather widget).

## Not included
Fully clear or blurred taskbars: on Windows 11 they need code injected into Explorer, which this app doesn't do.

## Install
Click "Install now" in Settings, or download `WallpaperManager-3.2.0-setup.exe` (no administrator rights needed) or the zip. Installing over an older version keeps your data.

## Tested
Windows 11 23H2, one display: unit and UI tests; widgets on the real desktop over a picture and above a live wallpaper window started after them; the screensaver's slideshow and a live wallpaper window lent to it and given back; the screensaver program (`WallpaperManager.scr`) started by Windows on the installed app.
