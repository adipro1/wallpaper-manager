Wallpaper Manager 3.3.1: a fix for 3.3.0.

## Fixed
- Night sky and Live Earth had no picture in the Library after updating to 3.3.0. They get one now, at the next start.

## Install
Click "Install now" in Settings, or download `WallpaperManager-3.3.1-setup.exe` (no administrator rights needed) or the zip. Installing over an older version keeps your data. Everything new in 3.3 is in the [3.3.0 notes](https://github.com/adipro1/wallpaper-manager/releases/tag/v3.3.0).

## Tested
Windows 11 23H2: unit tests, a fresh start on an empty data folder (both wallpapers get their pictures, no warnings), and the update from 3.3.0 on a PC where the pictures were missing.
