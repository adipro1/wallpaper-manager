Wallpaper Manager 3.3 "Sky & Earth": the real night sky and the real Earth as live wallpapers, and ambient sound that follows the weather. Your library, playlists, schedule and settings carry over.

## New
- **Night sky** (in the Library): the sky over your city right now, worked out on your PC. About 5,000 real stars with their colors and twinkle, constellation lines and names, the Milky Way, the moon in its true phase, and Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn where they really are. It turns with the Earth, goes through dusk and dawn with the real sun, follows the moon across the sky, and shows the real clouds and fog. Shooting stars now and then. Settings: the direction to look, constellations, names, Milky Way, shooting stars, hills, moon size, star brightness, camera drift.
- **Live Earth** (in the Library): the Earth as a globe or a map with yesterday's real satellite picture from NASA (clouds and all), the line between day and night as it is right now, city lights on the night side, the sun's glint on the sea, the atmosphere's glow, your place, and the International Space Station with its track. Centered on your place, on the sunlit side, or turning slowly. The pictures (about 10 MB) are fetched once a day while it shows.
- **Ambient sound** (Settings > Ambient sound, or the tray): a soft sound in the background. "Follow the weather" plays a storm, rain, wind in snow or fog, crickets on warm clear nights, a fire on cold ones and a breeze by day. Or pick one: rain, a thunderstorm, wind, a breeze, ocean waves, a stream, a fireplace, crickets or brown noise. The sounds are made on your PC (nothing to download). They fade in and out, get quiet under music, and stop while the PC is locked, the screen is off, the screensaver shows or a game or film runs full screen. Off until you switch it on.

## Not included
- Choosing a different sound for each wallpaper: one sound setting covers everything for now.
- The settings of the two new wallpapers are in English in the German version.

## Install
Click "Install now" in Settings, or download `WallpaperManager-3.3.0-setup.exe` (no administrator rights needed) or the zip. Installing over an older version keeps your data. The two new wallpapers appear in your Library after the update.

## Tested
Windows 11 23H2, one display: unit tests (the moon's phases on known dates, eclipses, planets at opposition and greatest elongation, Polaris, the sun against the sunrise code; joining the satellite pictures; the sound choice, fades and seamless loops); the night sky drawn by Chromium at night, dusk, noon, full moon and in summer; Live Earth drawn with real NASA pictures and the real space station; the sound loops played by mpv.
