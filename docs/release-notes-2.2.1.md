A small fix for 2.2. Your library, playlists, schedule and settings carry over.

## Fixed
- **Logo**: the bottom-left corner of the logo stayed yellow whatever accent color you picked. It now takes a matching shade of your color (still gold with the default vermilion).

## Also
- The user guide that comes with the app mentions the accent color and one-click updates.

## Install
If you have 2.1 or 2.2, click "Install now" in Settings. Otherwise download `WallpaperManager-2.2.1-setup.exe` (no administrator rights needed) or the zip. Installing over an older version keeps your data.
