Wallpaper Manager 3.0 "Alive": your wallpaper follows the sun and the weather. Your library, playlists, schedule and settings carry over.

## New
- **Day and night** (Library > a picture > "Make it alive…"): the same picture turns golden at sunset, blue at dusk and dark at night, with lit windows and twinkling stars, following the real sun for your city. Or pick a time of day yourself.
- **Living weather**: when it rains, snows, gets foggy or storms outside, it does on your picture too, with lightning in a thunderstorm and darker nights. The weather comes from open-meteo.com for your city (free, no account, every 15 minutes, only while such a wallpaper shows; can be switched off in Settings).
- **Picture transitions**: when the desktop picture changes (by hand, from a playlist or the schedule) it **folds away like paper**, fades, burns away or ripples, instead of jumping. Settings > Live wallpapers > Picture changes. (Needs the web engine; off with "reduce motion".)
- **Dynamic themes**: Playlists > "Import dynamic theme…" reads WinDynamicDesktop `.ddw` themes. Their pictures follow the sun through the day, with the new transitions between them. Any playlist can do it too: choose "Follow the sun" and how many of its pictures are for the day.
- Shaders can use the real sky: `iSun` (the sun's height and direction), `iWeather` (rain, snow, fog, storm) and `iClouds`.

## Install
If you have 2.1 or newer, click "Install now" in Settings. Otherwise download `WallpaperManager-3.0.0-setup.exe` (no administrator rights needed) or the zip. Installing over an older version keeps your data.

Windows 11 23H2, one display: unit and UI tests, the effects and transitions compiled and played in the real Chromium behind the desktop icons, the sun's position against published tables, and real weather from open-meteo.com.
